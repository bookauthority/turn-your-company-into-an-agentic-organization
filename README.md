**Companion code and practical exercises for _Turn Your Company Into An Agentic Organization: Cut Your Operating Expenses By 80% With AI Agents_.**

**Book author:** Juan Cabrera

## About the book

This book provides a practical, implementation-focused roadmap for transforming traditional companies into agentic organizations by deploying coordinated AI agents and Multi-Agent Systems (MASes) to perform labor-intensive, repetitive, and cross-functional business operations. It explains how to identify high-cost operational bottlenecks, map existing workflows, redesign processes around autonomous and human-supervised agents, and orchestrate specialized agents across customer service, sales operations, finance, accounting, procurement, HR administration, marketing, IT support, compliance, reporting, and knowledge management. Readers learn to design production-ready agent architectures with task planning, tool use, integrations, shared state, durable workflows, approvals for high-risk actions, exception handling, audit trails, security controls, monitoring, and recovery mechanisms. The book includes realistic end-to-end business case studies, companion implementations, deployment patterns, and financial models that measure baseline labor and process costs against automation costs, accuracy, throughput, and service quality. It critically evaluates whether reductions of up to 80% in selected operating expenses are achievable, under what assumptions, and where human oversight remains essential. Emphasis is placed on phased adoption, workforce transition, governance, operational resilience, and demonstrable business value rather than unverified savings claims.

## Who this is for

Business owners, CEOs, COOs, operations directors, transformation leaders, department managers, enterprise architects, software engineers, and AI automation practitioners seeking to modernize company operations with multi-agent systems, reduce avoidable operating costs, and implement measurable, reliable AI-driven workflows.

1. Read the chapter you want to explore in the book.
